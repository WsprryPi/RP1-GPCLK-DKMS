# Phase 5.53 final control construction prompt

## Objective

Construct the final unauthorized lifecycle controls from the exact final
product archive, retained inactive target snapshot, installed product ledger,
and current qualification archive. Reconstruct every path-bearing consumer
from those artifact closures, seal the qualification root, and fake-exercise
all 38 lifecycle attempts and every same-version recovery boundary.

## Required execution

1. Verify the product archive, qualification archive, snapshot, ledger, and
   transferred representative-build identities before generating anything.
2. Derive installed paths and bytes from the split archive layouts. Do not copy
   a historical package graph or resolve executable bytes from a mutable
   checkout.
3. Bind a schema-4 fresh qualification identity and schema-7 removed-ledger
   pre-root envelope. Keep the same-version plan outside the envelope to avoid
   a hash cycle.
4. Reconstruct the sealed root and validate the route decision, bootstrap,
   target plan, execution instance, attempt index, every attempt document, and
   same-version driver input. Exercise the full fake-system package path and
   every same-version interruption boundary.
5. If reconstruction finds a missing archive-owned consumer, stop control
   construction, repair ownership, reproduce the qualification artifact twice,
   and restart generation from that new closure.
6. Keep all authority fields false and `executionReady` false. Perform a
   separate adversarial assessment, correct every actionable finding, rerun
   affected checks, then commit and push attributable changes.

## Non-goals and safety boundary

Do not contact or stage the target. Do not run the pre-root transition or any
lifecycle attempt. Do not install, remove, load, bind, unbind, or unload a
module or overlay; reboot; change GPIO, clocks, or DMA; connect a transmitter or
antenna; transmit; or produce RF.

## Exit criteria

Two generations are byte-identical; independent validators pass; every sealed
path resolves from the new archive/control closures; no historical control was
changed; authority remains false; and the next gate is exact operator
authorization for read-only recapture and offline regeneration only.
