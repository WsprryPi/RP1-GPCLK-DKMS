# Phase 5.12 calibrated-review relationship adversarial assessment

## Scope and method

This separate offline assessment reviewed the Phase 5.12 prompt, module and
packaging contracts, release decision, packaged policy, inventory, and
validator. It attempted to falsify authorization separation, Experimental-
prerelease truthfulness, packaging claim ceilings, frozen-candidate identity,
manifest review, release immutability, route/mode independence, change
invalidation, and lifecycle-rerun requirements. No target, calibrated
instrument, public release, or consuming repository was accessed or changed.

## Findings and reinjection

1. **Receiver-relative release shortcut:** Experimental still needs applicable
   lifecycle, integrity, review, enrollment, and Gate F prerequisites.
2. **Ambiguous frozen candidate:** the policy now binds commit, archive digest,
   UAPI, both overlays, manifest, package/tool identities, and expected tag.
3. **In-place promotion:** the prerelease is immutable; the final decision
   requires a newly reviewed manifest and separately reviewed identity.
4. **Incomplete invalidation:** behavior/source, overlays, UAPI, timing,
   package/generated bytes, signing, compatibility, and lifecycle tooling all
   trigger a new candidate and affected lifecycle reruns.
5. **Qualification generalization:** GPIO4/GPIO20 and all four mode families
   remain separate, with lesser states for incomplete or failed rows.

The findings were reinjected into the prompt, decision, governing contracts,
machine-readable policy, and validator. Affected checks and the complete
offline suite were repeated.

## Final assessment

No remaining objective finding was identified within Phase 5.12's offline
scope. This review establishes policy only. It provides no target lifecycle,
calibrated frequency, power, spectrum, GPIO, transmission, RF, publication,
final-manifest, `Qualified`, or cross-repository evidence.
